How this works

  • Every question is multiple choice — one best answer, three plausible distractors.
  • Navigate freely, flag questions for review, and jump around with the question palette.
  • In study mode nothing is revealed until you press Check answer — until then you can switch options freely; after checking, the question locks. In exam mode you can change answers until you submit.
  • Answer every item — unanswered questions are scored as incorrect.
  • Submit to get this mock’s score (100 to 1000, threshold ≥ 720), a per-domain breakdown, and full explanations.
  • Your progress autosaves in this browser. Shortcuts: A–D or 1–4 answer · ←/→ navigate · F flag.
